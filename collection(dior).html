Sauvage Fragrance Collection

In contrast, Dior's Sauvage Collection refers to a men’s fragrance line that draws inspiration from the freedom and untamed spirit of the outdoors . The collection includes variations such as Eau de Toilette, Eau de Parfum, Parfum, and Extrait formulations, as well as skincare products under the same branding. The concept of "sauvage" here is expressed through raw freshness, vibrant notes, and a sense of adventure, with fragrances designed to evoke masculinity, power, and connection to natural elements.
